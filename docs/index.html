Why LEPISZCZE

One benchmark.
A wider field of view.

Progress in language models should be visible across more than one narrow task. LEPISZCZE combines established Polish benchmarks with new datasets to test how models generalize across domains, structures and prediction problems.

01

Broad by design

Classification, sequence labeling, entailment, sentiment, paraphrase, named entities, part-of-speech tagging, punctuation restoration and extractive question answering in one evaluation framework.

NERSentimentPOSEntailmentQ&A
02

Reproducible

Versioned pipelines, fixed experiment configurations and tracked runs make the evaluation process inspectable rather than opaque.

03

Extensible

Models, datasets and tasks are represented as configuration-driven pipeline stages, lowering the cost of extending the benchmark.

LEPISZCZE · Question answering

Extractive QA, inside LEPISZCZE.

Three Polish SQuAD 2.0-style datasets are part of the evolving LEPISZCZE benchmark, adding answerable, paraphrased and unanswerable questions. Developed through Expansio, they joined LEPISZCZE after the original 2022 publication.

Future work · Legal AI

From Polish NLP
to legal intelligence.

LEPISZCZE is expanding toward specialized Legal AI evaluation. JuDDGES and AITAX are active projects expected to contribute new Polish legal datasets, tasks and reproducible benchmark protocols.

Future benchmark Judicial decisions

JuDDGES

Judicial Decision Data Gathering, Encoding, and Sharing combines court-decision datasets with human-in-the-loop annotation, structured information extraction and evaluation across Polish and England & Wales case law.

Planned LEPISZCZE contribution Legal information extraction · judgment analysis · cross-jurisdiction evaluation
Explore JuDDGES ↗
Future benchmark Tax law · 2024–2027

AITAX

An active AI tax-advisory project covering factual-state definition, semantic retrieval of similar cases, authority-position summarization and generation of draft tax-interpretation requests.

Planned LEPISZCZE contribution Tax-case retrieval · legal summarization · grounded document drafting
Explore AITAX ↗
Roadmap, not current results

JuDDGES and AITAX are not included in the current leaderboard. They will become benchmark components after their datasets, task definitions, metrics and versioned evaluation protocols are published.
